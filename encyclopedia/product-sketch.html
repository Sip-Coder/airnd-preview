A small product sketch

A product sketch shows one person doing one job on one screen, before anyone writes a platform.

Maybe you do want a product. Start smaller than your pride. One person: a shop owner, a teacher, you. One job: turn a messy note into a clean checklist, or show a visitor the next open seat. One screen where that job is obvious.

AI can draft the screen copy, suggest what the button says, and even outline the sad paths: empty state, error, too long, too short. You still decide what the product refuses to do. A sketch that refuses things is more serious than a sketch that promises a platform.

Bring the sketch to the form if you want company while you cut it down. I like this part. It is where history turns into something a stranger can click.
